# FinSight: Explainable Credit Risk Analytics and Loan Decision Support System

## Project Abstract

FinSight is an explainable credit risk analytics and loan decision support system designed to classify loan applicants as good or bad credit risks. The project uses the UCI Statlog German Credit dataset, which contains 1,000 applicant records described by 20 financial, personal, and credit-related attributes.

The system will use a reproducible data pipeline for data acquisition, validation, preprocessing, and analysis. Multiple machine-learning classification models will be trained and evaluated using metrics such as precision, recall, F1-score, ROC-AUC, and confusion matrices. The evaluation will also consider the unequal costs of classification errors, since approving a high-risk applicant may be more costly than rejecting a low-risk applicant.

Explainable AI techniques such as SHAP will be used to show the factors influencing the model's overall behaviour and individual predictions. A Streamlit dashboard will present applicant details, predicted risk, model confidence, important contributing factors, and analytical visualisations in an accessible format. FinSight is intended to support transparent and informed credit assessment while keeping the final lending decision under human supervision.
